2 · Height layers

You are marking, for every part of the map, how high it sits. Bigger number = higher platform. The marble rolls downhill and falls off edges.
VOID = off the map / a pit (marble dies). WALL = a rail, post, or block that stops the marble. The rest are floor heights.
Pick a layer, then paint the matching areas of the map.
